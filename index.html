About Us

Kampala Children of Hope Initiative is a developing community initiative focused on helping children access basic necessities, educational support and opportunities to grow with dignity and hope.

Our Work

🍲 Food Support

Providing essential food support to children and families facing difficult circumstances.

📚 Education

Helping children with school supplies and other educational necessities.

👕 Clothing & Hygiene

Supporting children with clothing, shoes and essential hygiene items.
