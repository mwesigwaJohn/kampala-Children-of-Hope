Who we are

Every child deserves a chance.

Kampala Children of Hope Initiative (KCOHI) is a community-focused initiative being developed to support children in Central Kampala.

🍲

Food Support

Providing food and essential supplies to children and families facing hardship.

📚

Education

Helping children access school materials and other educational necessities.

👕

Clothing & Hygiene

Providing clothing, shoes and basic hygiene essentials where needed.

How we work

Small steps. Real accountability.

We want supporters to know what their contribution is helping accomplish.

01 — Identify

Work with families and community representatives to identify children who need support.

02 — Support

Provide practical assistance based on the child's and family's needs.

03 — Report

Keep records of donations and expenses and share project updates with supporters.
