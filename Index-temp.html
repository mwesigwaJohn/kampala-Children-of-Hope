About Us

Kampala Children of Hope is a community-focused foundation dedicated to supporting children who need care, encouragement, education, basic necessities, and opportunities to grow.

We believe every child deserves to feel valued, supported, protected, and given an opportunity to reach their potential.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To support children through education, basic needs, community support, mentorship, and opportunities that help build a brighter future.

Our Vision

A future where every child has the opportunity to grow, learn, feel valued, and reach their full potential.

What We Do

📚 Education Support

Helping children access learning opportunities and school supplies.

🍲 Basic Needs

Supporting children with essential needs such as food and clothing.

❤️ Child Support

Providing encouragement, care, mentorship, and community support.

🤝 Community Outreach

Working with communities to support children who need assistance.

🏫 School Supplies

Helping provide useful educational materials to children in need.

🌱 Future Opportunities

Helping young people develop skills and hope for their future.
